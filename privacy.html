1. Information You Submit

HowToCancel does not require user accounts or login passwords to browse guides. When you voluntarily use our forms, we only receive the fields you enter—such as a service name, cancellation trap steps, an issue category and description, or a review (including an optional name/handle and rating).

This information is used solely to review accuracy, improve cancellation guides, fix site issues, and respond to helpful feedback.

2. Browser Storage (localStorage)

To keep the site fast and resilient, your browser may save a local copy of submissions in localStorage under keys such as howtocancel_submitted_traps, howtocancel_reported_issues, and howtocancel_user_reviews.

Those entries stay on your own device unless sent through the submission form, and you can clear them anytime through your browser site settings or developer tools.

3. Form & CDN Services

Form submissions are posted to Web3Forms (https://api.web3forms.com/submit) so reports and reviews can reach the site maintainer for review. Page styling and icons also load standard front-end assets from public CDNs such as jsDelivr.

HowToCancel does not sell or rent user-submitted feedback to data brokers.

4. Do Not Share Account or UPI Details

Please never enter passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs, bank account numbers, card numbers, PAN/government IDs, or private personal information in any form or comment on HowToCancel. Share only general subscription steps or website feedback.

5. Retention & Policy Updates

Submitted notes may be kept in inbox records or local browser storage as long as needed to maintain accurate guides or track site fixes. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the site evolves, with changes reflected on this page.

6. Contact & Removal Requests

If you have a privacy question or want a submitted note or reference reviewed or removed, contact us directly.
