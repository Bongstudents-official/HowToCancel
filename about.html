Built for people who want simple exits without hidden traps.

Cancelling a subscription or stopping an unwanted auto-debit should never require hunting through buried menus or guessing whether a plan actually stopped.

Why HowToCancel Exists

Many apps and services make signing up take two taps while hiding cancellation behind support tickets, retention loops, or separate billing portals. Even after you cancel inside an app, active UPI Autopay mandates or recurring card charges can catch you off guard.

HowToCancel gathers practical, step-by-step exit paths and billing warnings in one clean, searchable place so everyday users can stay in control of their money.

What You Will Find Here

  • Direct Cancellation Steps: Clear walkthroughs without filler or sales pitches.
  • Hidden Trap Alerts: Early warnings on auto-renewals, lock-in periods, and UPI Autopay mandates.
  • Community Corrections: Fast updates when services change menus or billing screens.
