1. Helpful Purpose & Intent

HowToCancel is created strictly to help everyday users locate public cancellation options, understand subscription billing terms, and check active UPI Autopay mandates or recurring payments.

This project is designed for consumer assistance and educational clarity—never to harm, disrupt, defame, or interfere with any company, app, bank, or service provider.

2. Non-Affiliation & Trademarks

HowToCancel is an independent community website and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially connected to any brand, application, payment gateway, or company mentioned in our guides.

All product names, logos, service marks, and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used solely to identify the services covered by each user guide.

3. No Guarantee & Billing Checks

Apps, websites, customer support flows, and payment apps update their screens and billing rules frequently. While we aim for clear and accurate walkthroughs, steps may change without notice and are provided on an "as is" basis for general guidance.

Always confirm that your cancellation or mandate revocation succeeded inside your official account settings, billing email/receipts, or payment app before assuming auto-renewal has stopped. Nothing on this site constitutes formal legal or financial advice.

4. Community Submissions & Terms

By using HowToCancel or submitting a cancellation trap, problem report, or review, you agree to share factual, respectful descriptions based on genuine user experience—avoiding spam, abusive language, personal data, or unverified accusations.

Submitted reports are reviewed before being published or used to update a guide. You must not attempt to scrape, overload, or misuse the website or its forms.
